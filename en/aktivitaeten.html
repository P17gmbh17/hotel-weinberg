01 Hiking

Castles, forests and blossoming valleys

From the Three Castles trail linking Hocheppan, Boymont and Korb to the blossoming Frühlingstal between the Montiggl Lakes and Lake Caldaro, the trails start right at the door.

The historic Mendel funicular with its Mount Roen summit, the Leuchtenburg ruin above Lake Caldaro, and the quiet Way of the Cross up to the Gleif church with its Kreuzstein lookout are all within a 10-25 minute drive.

02 Biking

Two wheels through the vineyards

The cycle path along the former Bolzano–Eppan–Kaltern railway line runs right past the hotel's own vineyards, flat, family-friendly, with sweeping views of the Dolomites.

Those wanting to go further can follow the Adige Valley cycle path all the way to Merano, or ride the Mitterberg loop from Eppan via Lake Caldaro and Auer to Bolzano, nearly all flat, through orchards and old farmsteads.

03 Golf

Tee off with a view of the castle

Golf Club Eppan "Blue Monster" is just a few minutes' drive away, a 9-hole course with water hazards and mountain views, next to the Schloss Freudenstein training centre with its practice course, driving range and putting green.

For longer rounds, the 9-hole course in Lana and the 18-hole Golf Club Dolomiti on Mendel Pass are within about a 30-45 minute drive.

04 Culture

Three castles, a thousand years of history

Hocheppan Castle with its Romanesque chapel, Boymont Castle and Korb Castle line the ridge above the vineyards.

Further afield, Sigmundskron Castle houses the Messner Mountain Museum Firmian: the history of mountaineering with a panoramic view over Bolzano. And right next door in St. Pauls, the St. Pauls winery opens its doors: for a guided walk through the Paulsnerfeld vineyards or the "Preclarus" tour through its WWII sparkling-wine bunker.

05 Swimming

Lakes to unwind by

The freely accessible Montiggl Lakes and Lake Caldaro with its beach lidos are both about 15-20 minutes away by car, perfect for an afternoon by the water.

For a full day out, drive over Mendel Pass or through the Adige Valley to Lake Garda, Riva del Garda and Torbole with their lakeside promenades, windsurfers and the Monte Baldo cable car.
