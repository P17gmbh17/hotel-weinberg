Privacy Policy

Protecting your personal data is important to us. This privacy policy explains which data we collect when you visit this website or make a room enquiry, and how we handle it.

Server log files

When you visit this website, technical information (e.g. browser type, operating system, IP address, date and time of access) is automatically collected by our hosting provider. This data is used solely for technical stability and security and is not combined with other data sources.

Contact and enquiry form

If you contact us via the enquiry form or by email, we process the data you provide (name, email address, travel dates, message) exclusively to handle your enquiry and, where applicable, for contract fulfilment. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Booking system

For room bookings we use the external booking system Booking Südtirol (bookingsuedtirol.com), embedded as a widget on our website. The data you enter (travel dates, contact details, any payment data) is transmitted directly to the provider and is subject to its own privacy policy.

Google Fonts

This website loads the "Tenor Sans" typeface from Google's servers (fonts.googleapis.com). This may establish a connection to servers of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited and transmit your IP address.

Google Maps

Individual pages link to Google Maps to make it easier to find our house. Clicking such a link will take you away from our website; Google's privacy policy applies.

Cookies

For details on the cookies used on this website, please see our Cookie Settings.

Your rights

You have the right at any time to access, rectify, erase or restrict the processing of your personal data, as well as a right to object to processing. Please contact info@hotelweinberg.eu. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority: for Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

Changes

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy as necessary to reflect changes in the law or to our offering.
