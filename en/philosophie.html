Mediterranean Living in St. Pauls

A small house, a big garden, plenty of palm trees – and a family who welcomes you personally.

Concept & History

Personally run, for generations

Surrounded by vineyards, Hotel Weinberg sits in St. Pauls on the South Tyrolean Wine Route: where the mild, Mediterranean climate brings together palm trees, wine and wide open views.

We're not a big resort. Here, people know each other, greet one another over the garden fence, and breakfast tastes of real, regional ingredients. A house with history, which we're carefully renewing step by step, without ever losing its personal character.

Our philosophy

What "Mediterranean Living" means to us

Palm trees instead of grandeur. Peace instead of hustle. A glass of South Tyrolean wine in the evening, the view of the Dolomites, the chirping of crickets in the garden. A Mediterranean way of life, at home on the South Tyrolean Wine Route: simple, honest, and with open arms.

Personality

Friendly, lively, always with heart

There's always someone from the family with you throughout your stay, with a warm smile, genuine hospitality and a sense for what's needed in the moment.

Whether it's a recommendation for the evening, a quick chat at reception, or simply a smile in passing: we're there when you need us, just like at home.
